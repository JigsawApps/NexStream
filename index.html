NexStream Privacy Policy Effective date: 5 July 2026 NexStream ("the app") is a media player for Apple devices published by Jigsaw Apps. This policy explains what information the app handles and where it goes. The short version: we do not collect any data from you at all. Data we collect None. NexStream contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no account system. We have no servers, and the app never transmits any information to us or to any third party on our behalf. Data stored on your device To make the app work, the following is stored locally on your device only: Playlists you add — the URLs, names, and settings of playlists you configure. Server credentials — if you add an Xtream Codes playlist, your username and password are stored in the iOS Keychain, Apple's encrypted credential store. They are used solely to connect to the server you specified. Parental control PIN — stored in the iOS Keychain. Viewing data — favourites, watch history, resume positions, and recordings are stored in the app's private storage so features like "Continue Watching" work. Deleting the app permanently removes all of this data. You can also erase it in-app via Settings. Network connections the app makes The app connects only to servers you configure: the playlist, stream, and programme-guide (EPG) URLs you add, and the artwork URLs referenced inside your playlists. Those servers can see your IP address and standard connection metadata, as with any internet connection; their handling of that data is governed by their own privacy policies, not ours. The optional demo playlist connects to publicly available streams from their respective broadcasters and the Blender Foundation. Purchases The optional NexStream Pro upgrade is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's own privacy policy. Children NexStream is rated 17+ and is not directed at children. The app includes PIN-protected parental controls. Changes to this policy If a future version of the app changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised before that version is released.
